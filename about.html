Who we are

Your neighbourhood pharmacy, run properly.

Not a startup chasing a trend — a pharmacy counter that's been filling prescriptions and answering questions long before "online ordering" was a phrase anyone used.

Our story

Built one prescription at a time

Ellahie Buksh & Sons started as a single counter, stocked and run the way a pharmacy should be — genuine medicine, honest pricing, and a pharmacist who actually knows what's in stock.

That hasn't changed. What has changed is how you can reach us — this catalog is the same shelf you'd find in person, just searchable, and an order is now one WhatsApp message away instead of a trip across town.

We don't warehouse guesswork. Every product listed here is something we've actually stocked, checked, and would hand across our own counter.
